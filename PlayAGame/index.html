Face/Off

A high-speed, high-stakes game of trivia for two to six players. Each player starts with a fixed amount of time and must answer questions to stop their clock and start their opponent's. The first player to run out of time is eliminated from the game.

A licence is required to run this game. Contact royal_flush on the Discord to get one; you'll need a valid Twitch account to validate it.
